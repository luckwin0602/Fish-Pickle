About Us

Traditional Pickle Making Since 1985

Golden Fish Pickles has been crafting premium fish pickles in Chinnamuttom for over 35 years. Our commitment to quality, traditional recipes, and fresh ingredients has made us the choice of pickle enthusiasts across the region.

Each jar is prepared with care, using only the finest fish and spices, ensuring an authentic taste that takes you back to tradition with every bite.
